# Personal website

- Scope: About, Research, Another Me, and the shared navigation and footer.
- Visitor mode: Experience, with Read taking priority on Research.
- Audience and job: Academic peers, collaborators, students, and other visitors need to identify Shuhuai Zhang, inspect his work, contact him, or explore selected performances.
- Content and proof: Use only the biography, publication records, working papers, external links, performance credits, and real video embeds in `content/`.
- Constraints: Keep Markdown maintenance, horizontal navigation, 16:9 video alignment, semantic HTML, responsive behavior, and no invented claims or decorative filler.
- Chosen direction: “Two Registers, One Line.” Low-saturation slate-blue and mineral-blue academic pages share a horizontal structural line with an aubergine-and-apricot music page. About is one full-height portrait canvas: transparent horizontal navigation, name, biography, and email all stay inside the protected left extension, while the right-side portrait remains unobstructed. Research and Another Me retain their established fields. Sans-led academic typography gives way to a measured serif register for performance titles without becoming a separate brand.
- Memorable moment: A visitor reads the complete identity and biography inside the quiet extension of one painterly portrait; the same horizontal line becomes the research index and the performance chronology.
- Execution: Code-led. Motion is limited to a single line-draw/page-arrival gesture and direct link states, with reduced-motion fallback.
