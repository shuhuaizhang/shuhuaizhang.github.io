# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary visitors are academic peers, prospective collaborators, students, and other people looking for Shuhuai Zhang's research or contact information. They should be able to understand his academic identity quickly, scan his research record, and optionally explore his work as a violinist.

## Product Purpose

This is Shuhuai Zhang's personal website. It presents a concise biography, research publications and working papers, selected violin performances, and a stable contact address. Success means that visitors can find the relevant page and understand its contents without explanation or promotional copy.

## Positioning

The site presents two genuine parts of one person: a behavioral economist studying artificial intelligence and an active violinist. The academic and musical pages should feel related without flattening their different subject matter.

## Operating Context

The site is a small static website published with GitHub Pages. Routine maintenance happens by editing `content/profile.md`, `content/research.md`, or `content/performances.md`; the build script turns those files into the About, Research, and Another Me pages.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the three-page structure: About, Research, and Another Me.
- Keep navigation horizontal on both desktop and mobile; it must not consume a permanent left rail.
- Keep the homepage limited to the name, contact address, and biography including research interests.
- Keep Research content-forward and easy to scan; do not add decorative numbering, badges, or invented metadata.
- Keep every performance video at a consistent 16:9 aspect ratio and aligned with its description.
- Preserve the Markdown-based maintenance workflow and existing external research and video links.
- Do not invent biographical claims, research results, performances, statistics, testimonials, or roles.

## Brand Commitments

Use the name “Shuhuai Zhang.” The voice is concise, factual, and first-person where the existing biography uses first person. Avoid generic portfolio conventions such as role labels, dashboard-like statistics, decorative monograms, floating cards, meaningless abstract graphics, sticker details, and ornamental interface chrome. Another Me may be more expressive than the academic pages, but it remains part of the same site.

## Evidence on Hand

- Biography and contact information: `content/profile.md`
- Publications and working papers with real outbound links: `content/research.md`
- Performance credits and real embedded recordings: `content/performances.md`
- No portrait, logo, testimonial, or additional visual asset is currently part of the site; future work must not fabricate one.

## Product Principles

1. Let real content establish identity.
2. Make academic information effortless to scan and verify.
3. Give the musical work a distinct register without splitting the identity.
4. Keep routine updates possible through the three Markdown files.
5. Prefer deliberate composition over decorative UI devices.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic headings and landmarks, keyboard-visible focus, a skip link, reduced-motion support, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and descriptive titles for embedded performance videos.
